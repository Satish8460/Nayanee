# Nayanee (ನಯನಿ) — Project Context & Master Record

**Product Identity:** Adaptive Vision Intelligence  
**Hackathon Target:** ZEISS — AI/ML for Advanced Frugal Innovation (AFI)  
**Core Mission:** Build an accessible, multilingual, smartphone-based adaptive vision-profiling system for optical retail and rural/resource-constrained environments, bridging the communication gap between everyday customers and eye-care professionals (optometrists/opticians).

---

## Development Status: All 6 Iterations Completed & Validated

- **Iteration 1 (P0):** Problem & Architecture Specification, POC-01 (Adaptive Engine), POC-02 (Speech Harness).
- **Iteration 2:** Multilingual Conversational Foundation (6 languages: Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, English).
- **Iteration 3:** Adaptive Intelligence & Functional Vision Profiling (Entropy scoring, contradiction sentinel, red-flag emergency triage).
- **Iteration 4:** Optometrist Workflow & Real-World Integration (Triage dashboard, EHR export, printable slip).
- **Iteration 5:** Reliability, Accessibility & Field Validation (Adversarial defenses, noise tolerance, mid-session language switching).
- **Iteration 6:** Product Hardening, Unified Launchers & Live Demos (`start.bat`, `run_tests.bat`, `demo_e2e.py`).
