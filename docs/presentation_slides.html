⚠️ The Rural & Retail Optical Dilemma
  • No Dedicated Equipment: Rural stores and vision centers have no autorefractors ($3K-$15K)—just a smartphone or a counter.
  • The 25-Question Form Trap: Static paper questionnaires fatigue walk-in customers with irrelevant questions.
  • Language & Literacy Barrier: Customers speak Indic dialects (Kannada, Hindi, etc.); English forms create high friction.
  • The Unsafe LLM Trap: Generic AI chatbots hallucinate prescriptions, lack clinical boundary checks, and require expensive GPUs.
💡 The Nayanee Solution (AFI Ethos)
  • Smartphone-Native Anamnesis: Captures complete functional vision demands without any specialized optical hardware.
  • Voice-First in 6 Indic Languages: Natural Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, and English clinical conversation.
  • Deterministic Clinical Triage: Distinguishes functional needs from medical red flags; never hallucinates or pretends to diagnose.
  • EHR & Optometrist Ready: Instantly prepares optometrist handover with printable consultation slip and lens design guidance.

1. Information-Theoretic Adaptive Engine (POC-01)
  • Shannon Entropy Scoring: Quantifies remaining uncertainty across 5 vision domains (Distance, Near, Screen, Lifestyle, Eyewear).
  • Dynamic Pruning: Automatically suppresses non-applicable questions (e.g. progressive adaptation for first-time readers).
  • Cross-Check Contradiction Detector: Catches inconsistent answers in real-time and flags for optometrist review.
2. Voice Architecture & Zero-GPU SLU (POC-02 & 03)
  • Server-Streamed Native MP3 Audio: Streams high-clarity native Indic voice (/api/v1/session/speak), overcoming OS speech pack deficits.
  • Clinical Spoken Language Understanding: Zero-GPU keyword and token-overlap parser matches intent with confidence scoring.
  • Deterministic Red-Flag Sentinel: Immediate halt on acute symptoms (pain, flashes, vision drop); zero LLM hallucination.
